MathX is a powerful math and game-development extension for MakeCode Arcade that provides a collection of utility functions for movement, animation, physics, vectors, interpolation, timing, and advanced calculations. The extension includes tools for interpolation, easing, trigonometry, angle and distance calculations, vectors, springs, dampening, oscillation, prediction, orbiting, timing, and more. Adds the `Vectors` and `MathX` category with 16 subcategories: `Motion`, `Physics`, `Effects`, `Vector`, `Movement`, `Logic`, `Utility`, `Geometry`, `Time`, `Juice`, `Trigonometry`, `Randomness`, `Advanced Math`, `Custom Functions`, and `Number Theory`.
