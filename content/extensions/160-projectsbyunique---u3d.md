A raycasting engine that renders a 3D voxel world from a heightmap and colormap image pair, with tilemap-based textured walls. The demo is very impressive; it is recommended that you check it out to see what this extension is fully capable of! Adds a `U3D` category to the toolbox. As of September 5, 2026, this extension does not seem to work (blockly workspace gets corrupted) anymore.
