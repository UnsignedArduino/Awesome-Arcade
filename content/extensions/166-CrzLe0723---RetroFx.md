RetroFX+ is a utility extension for MakeCode Arcade that includes customizable UI systems, dialog boxes, menus, sound utilities, visual effects, transitions, and other retro-inspired tools. Adds the Retro Fx+ category with 7 subcategories: `UI FX`, `Audio FX`, `System FX`, `Screen FX`, `Character FX`, `Combat FX`, `World FX`, `Retro FX`, and `Movement`. This extension depends on a lot of other extensions and will add many more categories to the toolbox.
